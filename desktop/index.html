Make yourself at home

The bottom taskbar shows your open windows. Click a window's name to switch to it, or use Alt + Tab. Use its corner buttons to minimise, maximise or close it.

Files opens the two-panel file manager. Click folders to open them; Tab switches panels, F5 copies, F6 moves and F10 closes it. Your home folder is /home/user.

Notes opens your personal text file. Press Ctrl + O, then Enter to write it, and Ctrl + X to close the editor.

Save documents inside their apps first. Autosave then copies your desktop files and settings to private storage. Wait for Saved at… or use Save and exit before leaving. Open programs start fresh on your next computer.
